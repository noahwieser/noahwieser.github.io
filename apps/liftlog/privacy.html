Lift Log

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Lift Log is a local-first app. It stores your workout data on your device and does not operate servers that receive that data. Data leaves the app only when you choose to export or share it, or when your device's operating system or backup provider includes app data in a device backup based on your settings.

Data Stored On Device

  • Workout templates, exercises, planned sets, and supersets
  • Active and completed workout history, including weights, repetitions, notes, and completion times
  • One-rep max entries and progress derived from your workout history
  • App settings, such as appearance, weight unit, and workout preferences

Export, Restore, And Sharing

  • The Export Data feature creates a JSON file containing your local Lift Log data, including workout history and app settings.
  • When you export or share that file, Lift Log uses your device's system share sheet. Lift Log does not choose the recipient or destination and does not retain a server copy.
  • Any app or service you select as the destination handles the exported file under its own privacy practices. Store exported files somewhere private and delete copies you no longer need.
  • The Restore Data feature reads only a Lift Log JSON file that you select and replaces the app's on-device data only after you confirm the restore.

Data We Do Not Collect Or Send

  • No account is required.
  • Lift Log does not automatically transmit workout data or app settings to Lift Log servers.
  • Lift Log does not provide cloud sync. Operating-system device backups are controlled by your device and account settings.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Third Parties And Tracking

  • Lift Log does not include ad SDKs.
  • Lift Log does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Lift Log does not track you across apps or websites.

iOS Live Activities Disclosure

If you use Lift Log on iOS with Live Activities enabled, active workout details (such as workout name and next set information) may appear on the lock screen or Dynamic Island. This information remains on-device but may be visible to someone who can view your screen.

Data Retention And Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete workout templates, logged workouts, and one-rep max entries in the app. Uninstalling the app may remove remaining local app data. Exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete them from those locations.

Children's Privacy

Lift Log is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the last updated date.
